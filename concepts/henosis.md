## Gloss / Divergence map

**Henōsis** (ἕνωσις, "oneness, union") is the **mystical union with the [[plotinus-one|One]]** — the culminating goal of the soul's journey in [[neoplatonism|Neoplatonism]]. It is the completion of the **return (*epistrophē*)**: having emanated (*proodos*) from the One through Nous and Soul, the soul reverses "the ontological process of consciousness," strips away all discursive thought and multiplicity, and **merges with the absolute, simple Source** (the One / the Good / the Monad).

**Beyond thought — past even Nous.** Because the One is beyond being and beyond the subject–object duality of thinking, henōsis is *not* an act of knowing: it lies **above the level of [[nous|Intellect]]**. One reaches "a blank state where the individual may grasp or merge with the One," through **self-purification and contemplation (*theōria*)** until individual consciousness dissolves into absolute simplicity. (Iamblichus held that contemplation alone is insufficient and that henōsis requires **theurgy** — ritual ascent — a methodological split within the school.)

**Reach and comparanda.** Henōsis became the template for the Christian doctrine of **theosis** (divinization) via Pseudo-Dionysius, and Wikipedia explicitly aligns it with Eastern liberation concepts — **mokṣa** and Sufi **fanāʾ** (self-annihilation in the divine). These cross-tradition parallels are *real and instructive but not identities* — see the NOT-equivalent below; the Christian/Sufi comparanda are noted in prose for want of nodes.
