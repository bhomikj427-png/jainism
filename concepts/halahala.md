## Gloss / Divergence map

**Hālāhala** (also **Kālakūṭa**, "poison of death") is the **deadly poison that rises during the [[samudra-manthana|Samudra-Manthana]]** — before the *amṛta*, the churning first throws up a toxin whose fumes threaten to destroy devas and asuras alike. They appeal to **[[shiva|Śiva]]**, who **drinks the poison** to save creation; his consort grips his throat so it cannot pass into his body, and the toxin stains his throat blue — the origin of the epithet **Nīlakaṇṭha** ("blue-throated"). The episode is the standard etiology of one of Śiva's best-known names.

**Variants (recorded, minor):**

| point | reading | source |
|---|---|---|
| who reduces the poison | Śiva drinks the whole of it | primary account |
| — | Vāyu first lessens its potency, then Śiva drinks a portion | alternative |
| where it comes from | rises from the churned ocean | most texts |
| — | emitted by **[[vasuki|Vāsuki]]** the churning-serpent, drunk directly from him | variant |
