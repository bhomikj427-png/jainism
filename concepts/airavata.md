## Gloss / Divergence map

**Airāvata** is the **white elephant**, king of elephants, that rises as one of the **ratnas (treasures) of the [[samudra-manthana|Samudra-Manthana]]** and becomes the **mount (vāhana) of Indra**, lord of thunder and rain. He is described with **multiple heads (three or ten), tusks, and trunks**, and is linked to **clouds and rain** (the mythic elephants that draw up water and release it as precipitation — a natural fit for Indra's storm-rulership). He recurs in **Jain** cosmology as well, and became a royal emblem across Southeast Asia (Thailand, Laos).

This is a converged, low-controversy node: the elephant's churning-origin and Indra-mount role are stable across sources; only the head/tusk count varies (three vs ten).
