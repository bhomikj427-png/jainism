## Gloss / Divergence map

**Nous** (νοῦς, "intellect / mind / intelligence") is a central Greek philosophical term with a layered history; in this corpus it is primarily the **second hypostasis of [[neoplatonism|Neoplatonism]]**, but its earlier senses must be kept distinct:

| use | what Nous is |
|---|---|
| **Anaxagoras** (~500 BCE) | the **cosmic ordering Mind** that "arranged all other things in the cosmos in their proper order" and set them in rotating motion — an external orderer of pre-existing stuff |
| **Aristotle** | the **intellect** as the faculty grasping first principles; and, cosmically, the **Unmoved Mover** as *noēsis noēseōs*, "thought thinking itself," identified with God |
| **Plotinus / Neoplatonism** | the **second hypostasis** — the first emanation from the One, the realm of the Platonic Forms, where being and thinking coincide |

**The Plotinian Nous (the corpus sense).** In Plotinus' hierarchy **the One → Nous → Soul** (see [[plotinus-one]]), Nous is the **first emanation from the [[plotinus-one|One]]** and the highest *knowable* principle. It is "an image of God" that **thinks its own contents, which are thoughts equated to the Platonic Forms** — so the Forms are not a separate Platonic "place" but the very contents of divine Intellect. Crucially, in Nous **"being and thinking coincide"**: it is self-thinking thought, a *multiplicity-in-unity* (many Forms, one Intellect). It is therefore **below the One**, because the One is utterly simple and beyond the subject–object duality that thinking already introduces — to think is to be two (thinker and thought), so the absolutely simple One cannot be Nous.

**Why this matters for the corpus.** Nous is the precise pivot of the Neoplatonism–Advaita comparison: Advaita's **Brahman as pure consciousness (*cit*)** maps onto the Neoplatonic **Nous** (the level of self-luminous being-thinking), **not** onto the supra-conscious One — so "the Advaita highest is the Neoplatonic second-highest." This is exactly why the One↔Brahman identification is a NOT-equivalent (recorded in [[plotinus-one]]).
