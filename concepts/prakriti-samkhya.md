## Gloss / Divergence map

In Sāṃkhya, prakṛti is **the primal, unconscious, material principle** — the first and only material substance, from which ALL of the manifest world (both physical and mental) evolves. It is the "known" side of reality; puruṣa (pure consciousness) is the "knower" side. This hard two-substance dualism is structurally unlike any other Indian system.

**Three guṇas constitute prakṛti entirely:**

| guṇa | Sanskrit | qualities | role in creation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sattva | sattva | clarity, lightness, harmony, goodness | mind, intellect, luminosity |
| Rajas | rajas | activity, passion, movement, energy | motion, transformation, drives |
| Tamas | tamas | heaviness, inertia, darkness, ignorance | solidity, resistance, opacity |

When the three guṇas are in perfect equilibrium → *avyakta* (unmanifest prakṛti = pure potential, before creation). When disturbed (by proximity to puruṣa) → manifest creation begins as the guṇas become imbalanced.

**The 25 principles (tattvas) of Sāṃkhya** — all except puruṣa are evolutes of prakṛti:
prakṛti → *mahat/buddhi* (cosmic intelligence) → *ahaṃkāra* (ego-principle) → [sattva branch:] 5 *jñānendriyas* (sense organs) + 5 *karmendriyas* (action organs) + *manas* (mind) → [tamas branch:] 5 *tanmātras* (subtle elements: sound/touch/form/taste/smell) → 5 *mahābhūtas* (gross elements: ether/air/fire/water/earth). Puruṣa = 25th, not an evolute but the witness.

**Key: mind and intellect are PART of prakṛti** — not separate substances. In Jainism, there is no equivalent: manas is not a separate dravya (the TS lists 6 dravyas, none is "mind"). In Vaiśeṣika, manas is a ninth, separate dravya. In Sāṃkhya, manas, buddhi, and ahaṃkāra are all matter-functions.

## Structural comparison across traditions

| dimension | Sāṃkhya prakṛti | Jain pudgala | Vaiśeṣika physical dravyas |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is there one primal matter? | yes — one undivided prakṛti | no — all paramāṇus are discrete, equivalent, irreducible | no — four atomic types (earth/water/fire/air) |
| Does matter include mind? | yes — manas, buddhi, ahaṃkāra are evolutes of prakṛti | no — jīva (soul) is consciousness; pudgala is physical only | no — manas is a separate atomic dravya |
| How many ultimate principles? | 2 (prakṛti + puruṣa multiplied) | 6 dravyas (jīva + 5 ajīva) | 9 dravyas |
| Is matter atomic? | no — prakṛti is ONE continuous potential | yes — discrete paramāṇus | yes — four atomic types |
| Role of consciousness | puruṣa = pure passive witness; never acts | jīva = active doer/experiencer; karma binds it | ātman = substrate of consciousness; plural, active |
