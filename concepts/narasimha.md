## Gloss / Divergence map

**Narasiṃha** ("man-lion," *nara* + *siṃha*) is the **fourth of the [[avatara-vedanta|daśāvatāra]]** (after [[varaha|Varāha]], before [[vamana|Vāmana]]) — Viṣṇu's part-human, part-lion form. Its defining content is the **boon-loophole**: the asura king **Hiraṇyakaśipu** (brother of Varāha's Hiraṇyākṣa) wins from Brahmā a boon that he cannot be killed *by man or beast, by deva, indoors or outdoors, by day or by night, on the ground or in the sky, by any weapon.* To protect the boy-devotee **Prahlāda** — Hiraṇyakaśipu's own son, who insists Viṣṇu is everywhere, even in a palace pillar — Viṣṇu bursts from the pillar as Narasiṃha and **satisfies every clause by contradiction**:

| boon-clause | how Narasiṃha voids it |
|---|---|
| not by man or beast | he is **neither** — a man-lion |
| not by day or night | at **twilight** (saṃdhyā) |
| not indoors or outdoors | on the **threshold** of the hall |
| not on ground or in sky | on his **lap** |
| not by any weapon | with his **claws** (nails — no weapon) |

The avatāra is thus the theology of the **liminal**: God acts precisely in the categorial gaps the boon left open. Sources converge tightly on this structure; only minor details (exact wording of the clauses) vary by Purāṇa.
