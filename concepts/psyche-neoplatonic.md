## Gloss / Divergence map

**Psychē** (Soul) is the **third hypostasis** of Plotinian Neoplatonism, completing the descending triad One → Intellect ([[nous|Nous]]) → Soul. It proceeds from Nous as Nous proceeds from the One, by the "two-activities" doctrine: Soul is the outer activity of Intellect that turns back to contemplate its source while projecting images of the eternal Forms downward into matter. It is therefore the crucial **intermediary** between the intelligible and the sensible. Neoplatonists distinguish the cosmic **World-Soul** (which generates and animates the whole material universe through contemplation) from **individual souls** (animating particular organisms); each human soul is itself bipartite — a higher part perceiving the intelligible, a lower part caring for the body.

**Prime-directive cautions (distinctions the map must teach).** Soul here is a *metaphysical hypostasis*, an ontological level of reality — NOT the modern psychological "psyche" (mind/ego/personality), and NOT the individual embodied self ([[jiva]]) of Indian thought. And by the repo's Vedānta-mapping discipline, Brahman-as-*cit* maps to **Nous** (the second hypostasis), so Soul is **not** the analogue of Brahman.
