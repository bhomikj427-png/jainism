## Gloss / Divergence map

**Ketu** is the **severed body (tail)** of the asura **Svarbhānu** — the counterpart of **[[rahu|Rāhu]]**, his severed head — left when Viṣṇu beheaded the demon during the **[[samudra-manthana|Samudra-Manthana]]**. In jyotiṣa Ketu is a **graha** (one of the *navagraha*) identified with the **descending (south) lunar node**, sitting **180° opposite Rāhu** and sharing the ~18-year nodal cycle; it is held responsible for the lunar eclipse as Rāhu is for the solar. The Sanskrit *ketu* also means "comet / banner / sign," and Ketu carries older associations with **comets, meteors, and shadow-bodies** — a partly distinct stratum later absorbed into the node-identification.

The same §0 caution as Rāhu applies: Ketu names an **astronomical node-point**, but the demon-tail myth is not itself an eclipse theory; the node took the name, the etiology was correlated to it. Astrologically Ketu is read as **mokṣa-ward** — non-attachment, karmic residue, spiritual liberation — the inward pole to Rāhu's outward craving.
