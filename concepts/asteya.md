## Gloss / Divergence map

Asteya is **non-stealing** — the third of the five great vows (*mahāvratas*). In Jain usage it encompasses not only not taking what is not given but a comprehensive non-interference with any resource, space, or possession belonging to others.

**TS 7.6 Sanskrit:** *śūnyāgāravimocitāvāsaparoparodhākaraṇabhaikṣyaśuddhisadharmāvisaṃvādāḥ pañca* — "Residence in a solitary place, residence in a deserted habitation, causing no hindrance to others, acceptance of clean food, and not bickering with fellow monks are the five observances for the vow of non-stealing." (Vijay K. Jain 2018, Motilal Banarsidass; fetched directly.)

**The five observances (*bhāvanās*) for asteya:**

| # | Sanskrit | English | principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | śūnyāgāravāsa | living in vacant/unoccupied places (caves, tree hollows) | not displacing any being from its dwelling |
| 2 | vimocitāvāsa | residing only in places abandoned/released by others | not claiming space still in use |
| 3 | paroparodhākaraṇa | causing no hindrance to others | not obstructing others' movement, access, or use |
| 4 | bhaikṣyaśuddhi | accepting only clean food per scriptural guidelines | not taking food wrongly obtained or offered |
| 5 | sadharmāvisaṃvāda | avoiding disputes with fellow ascetics over possessions | not competing for resources even within the monastic community |

**Structural logic:** Each observance addresses a subtle form of appropriation — not just theft of property but territorial encroachment, obstruction, and competitive claim on shared resources. Asteya at the mahāvrata level is a total non-grasping for anything beyond what is freely given and properly obtained.

**Connection to kaṣāya:** Asteya directly targets *lobha* (greed) — the fourth kaṣāya. Taking what is not given is driven by greed; the observances train against the impulses that lead even to minor appropriation.

**Laypeople's minor vow:** Do not steal; do not receive stolen goods; do not encourage theft. The scope is narrowed to direct material theft.
