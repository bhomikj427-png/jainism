## Gloss / Divergence map

**Plotinus** (c. 204/5–270 CE; born in Roman Egypt, taught in Rome) is the founder of **Neoplatonism** — the systematic, mystical-metaphysical revival of Plato that dominated late-antique philosophy and shaped Christian, Islamic, and Jewish thought for a millennium. He studied eleven years under **Ammonius Saccas** in Alexandria; his pupil **Porphyry** edited his treatises into the **Enneads** ("nines": six groups of nine). Plotinus himself claimed only to be expounding Plato faithfully — "Neoplatonism" is a modern label.

**The three hypostases (the metaphysical core):**

| hypostasis | what it is |
|---|---|
| **The One** (*to Hen*) / the Good | the absolutely simple, self-sufficient first principle, **beyond being** and beyond all predicates — ineffable |
| **Intellect** (*Nous*) | the first emanation; the realm of the Platonic Forms as an organic "multiple unity" of self-thinking thought |
| **Soul** (*Psychē*) | the intermediate principle, mediating between the intelligible and the sensible, animating the cosmos |

**Emanation (procession and return).** Reality flows from the One by *proodos* (procession) — an eternal, necessary overflow that **does not diminish** the source, not a deliberate creation — through descending grades of decreasing unity. The complementary movement is *epistrophē* (return): each level, and especially the human soul, reverts toward its origin. The soul's culminating goal is **henōsis** — mystical union with the One — reached through virtue and contemplation.

**Why this matters for the corpus.** Plotinus is the Western pole of the cross-tradition emanation/return comparison: his "One → Nous → Soul → ascent back" maps *structurally* onto Advaita's "Brahman → graded reality → jñāna-return," which is exactly why the corpus draws both a `structurally-parallel-to` and an `often-conflated-with-NOT-equivalent` between Neoplatonism and Advaita (the One is *beyond* consciousness and *really* emanates; Brahman *is* consciousness and the world is *māyā*-appearance, not real procession).
