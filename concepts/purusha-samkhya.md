## Gloss / Divergence map

In Sāṃkhya, puruṣa is **pure, passive consciousness** — the "knower" side of the fundamental dualism (prakṛti = unconscious matter; puruṣa = conscious witness). Sāṃkhyakārikā 19 defines it directly: *"The soul is the conscious witness, separate, neutral, seer, and inactive."* (Cited in search-result aggregation from shahzadpurfarmyoga.com commentary.)

**Key properties of puruṣa:**
- **Pure witness** (*sākṣin*) — observes but never acts; never produces, transforms, or changes
- **Inactive** — puruṣa cannot act on prakṛti; its mere *proximity* disturbs the guṇa-equilibrium and triggers creation (like a magnet that does not touch but causes movement)
- **Eternal, unchanging** — puruṣa is beyond time, change, pleasure, and pain
- **No guṇas** — prakṛti consists of guṇas; puruṣa has none
- **Plural** — there are infinitely many puruṣas, each distinct individual consciousness (Sāṃkhya is pluralist about consciousness, unlike Advaita)

**The bondage mechanism:** Puruṣa does not actually become bound; bondage is an apparent state arising from *avidyā* (ignorance/false identification) — puruṣa mistakenly identifies with the body/mind complex (which is prakṛti). This is a *cognitive error*, not a physical bondage.

**Liberation (*kaivalya*):** When discrimination (*viveka-khyāti*) between puruṣa and prakṛti is achieved — seeing clearly that "I am not this body/mind/ego" — liberation arises. No karma-removal is needed; liberation is *recognition*, not physical purification.

## Comparison across "consciousness" concepts

| dimension | Sāṃkhya puruṣa | Jain jīva | Advaita ātman |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active or passive? | passive witness only; never acts | active doer and experiencer; karma binds it | pure awareness; liberation = recognition of Brahman-identity |
| How many? | infinitely many distinct puruṣas | infinitely many distinct jīvas | ultimately one (Brahman); apparent plurality is māyā |
| Is it really bound? | no — bondage is cognitive error/false identification | yes — karma particles physically adhere | no — bondage is avidyā (ignorance) |
| Liberation mechanism | *viveka-khyāti* (discrimination/recognition) | physical removal of all karma matter | *jñāna* (recognising ātman = Brahman) |
| Relation to matter | absolutely separate; never touched by prakṛti | interacts with pudgala; karma binds jīva | ultimately matter is māyā; ātman = Brahman is the only real |
| Has the consciousness *size*? | no — puruṣa is infinite/dimensionless | yes — jīva fills its body (svadeha-parimāṇa) | no size (Brahman is all-pervasive) |
