## Gloss / Divergence map

**Dvaita Vedānta** ("dualist Vedānta," also **Tattvavāda**, "the realist position") is the **thoroughgoing pluralist-realist** reading of Vedānta, founded by **[[madhva|Madhva]] / Madhvācārya** (conventionally 1238–1317 CE) of Udupi. It rests on his commentary (bhāṣya) on the [[brahma-sutra]]. Of the three great Vedānta sub-schools it is the **furthest from Śaṅkara's monism** ([[advaita-vedanta]]) and a step beyond Rāmānuja's qualified non-dualism ([[vishishtadvaita]]).

**The one thesis: difference is fundamental.** Reality divides into exactly **one independent entity (svatantra-tattva) — God, Viṣṇu** — and everything else as **dependent (paratantra)**: individual souls (**jīvas**) and matter (**jaḍa/prakṛti**). Madhva codifies this as the **pañca-bheda, the five eternal differences**: (1) God ≠ soul, (2) God ≠ matter, (3) soul ≠ soul, (4) soul ≠ matter, (5) matter ≠ matter. Difference, not sameness, is the structure of the real. Souls are **eternally co-eternal with God but utterly dependent on him** — "reflections" (**pratibimba**) of the original (**bimba**), never identical to it.

**Against Advaita.** The world is a **real creation of Brahman, not māyā/illusion**; an Advaita that explains plurality away by ignorance, Madhva argues, makes liberation logically impossible. The souls' individuality is permanent — retained even in liberation.

**Liberation.** *Mokṣa* is **eternal, blissful, individuality-retaining service to Viṣṇu**, attained by bhakti (with jñāna and karma yoga). Madhva uniquely teaches a **threefold gradation of souls** — those fit for liberation (sāttvika), those bound to eternal saṃsāra (rājasa), and those destined for **eternal damnation** (tāmasa) — a doctrine almost unparalleled in Indian thought.

## Reasoning across signals

**Signal 1** (Wikipedia, "Dvaita Vedanta"): Madhvācārya 1238–1317; Viṣṇu as sole independent reality (svatantra) vs all else dependent (paratantra); the five pañca-bheda differences (listed); rejection of Advaita māyā as making liberation impossible; mokṣa via bhakti + jñāna, not merger; three classes of souls including eternal damnation; alt. names Tattvavāda / Svatantra-Advitīya-Brahmavāda. **Signal 2** (New World Encyclopedia, "Madhva"): Madhva 1238–1317, Udupi; ~37 treatises incl. Brahmasūtra commentary; world a *real* creation of Brahman, not illusory; dependent (jīvas, prakṛti) vs one independent (Viṣṇu); the five eternal differences; souls co-eternal but dependent, bimba/pratibimba; individuality retained in mokṣa; liberation as eternal service.

The two are independent (a reference article and a separate encyclopedia) and agree on dating, the svatantra/paratantra split, the pañca-bheda, the rejection of māyā, the bimba/pratibimba soul-model, and the service-account of mokṣa. **Medium, not high**: Madhva's bhāṣya Sanskrit was not fetched verbatim; the corpus cross-check is structural (against [[jiva]]/[[advaita-vedanta]]), not text-level.
