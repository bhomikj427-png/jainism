## Gloss / Divergence map

**Varāha** is the **boar** — in classical Vaiṣṇavism the **third of the [[avatara-vedanta|daśāvatāra]]** (after [[kurma|Kūrma]], before [[narasimha|Narasiṃha]]). The Purāṇic myth: the demon **Hiraṇyākṣa** drags the earth-goddess **Bhūdevī (Pṛthvī)** down into the **cosmic ocean**; Viṣṇu takes boar-form, **slays Hiraṇyākṣa**, and **lifts the earth out on his tusks**, restoring her so creation can resume.

The same **diachronic identity-shift** seen on [[matsya|Matsya]] and [[kurma|Kūrma]] is here especially explicit (a recorded finding, not a source-dispute):

| layer | who the boar *is* | source |
|---|---|---|
| Vedic | a cosmic boar named **Emūṣa**, identified with **Prajāpati / Brahmā**, raises the hand-sized earth from the primordial waters | Taittirīya Saṃhitā / Brāhmaṇa; Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa |
| transitional | Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 14.1.2 **harmonizes** the two, conflating Emūṣa into Prajāpati | ŚB |
| Purāṇic | the boar is **Viṣṇu**, the Hiraṇyākṣa-slayer | Mahābhārata; Viṣṇu / Bhāgavata Purāṇas |

"In the Taittirīya Saṃhitā… and the Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa it is **Prajāpati** who saves the earth in his boar incarnation and **not Viṣṇu**" — the boar later "completes the full transition from Prajāpati-Brahmā to the avatar of Nārāyaṇa-Viṣṇu."

## Reasoning across signals

1. **Wikipedia, "Varaha"** — third of the daśāvatāra; Hiraṇyākṣa / Bhūmi rescue on the tusks; explicit ŚB / Taittirīya Prajāpati-boar (Emūṣa) and the later "full transition… to the avatar of Nārāyaṇa-Viṣṇu."
2. **Cultural Samvaad, "Varaha Avatar of Vishnu"** (independent) — corroborates the cosmic-ocean rescue and Hiraṇyākṣa, and states the Vedic priority of Prajāpati: the boar-rescue was Prajāpati's "a tradition that later evolved into the Viṣṇu-Varāha narrative."

Two independent sources agree on the rescue narrative **and** on the Prajāpati → Viṣṇu reattribution → **converged**, confidence **medium**.
