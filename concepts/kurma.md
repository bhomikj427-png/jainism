## Gloss / Divergence map

**Kūrma** is the **tortoise** — in classical Vaiṣṇavism the **second of the daśāvatāra** (after Matsya, before Varāha), the form Viṣṇu takes in the **[[samudra-manthana|Samudra-Manthana]]** to bear **Mount Mandara on his back** as the pivot of the churning. But the tortoise is **older than its Vaiṣṇava identity**, and that shift is the contested point:

| reading | who holds it | what it commits you to |
|---|---|---|
| Kūrma = avatāra of **Viṣṇu**, the churning-pivot | classical Purāṇic Vaiṣṇavism (Viṣṇu/Bhāgavata/Kūrma Purāṇas) | the standard daśāvatāra theology — God descends as tortoise |
| the cosmic tortoise = **Prajāpati / Brahmā** | Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa (Vedic) | an **earlier** cosmogonic tortoise; as Viṣṇu-worship rose, the Prajāpati-tortoise's role was **transferred** to Viṣṇu — a historical reattribution, not an original identity |

Treating the Vedic Prajāpati-tortoise and the Vaiṣṇava Kūrma-avatāra as one being **flattens a real diachronic shift** (Brāhmaṇa cosmogony → Purāṇic Vaiṣṇavism); they are continuous in image but not in theology.

## Reasoning across signals

1. **Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva (Ganguli tr.)** — the tortoise-king holds Mount Mandara on his back during the churning (the apparatus role; here a tortoise-king, the Vaiṣṇava identification firming up in the Purāṇas).
2. **Wikipedia, "Kurma"** — second of the daśāvatāra; churning-pivot; and explicitly the Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa **Prajāpati-tortoise** later transferred to Viṣṇu as Brahmā-worship waned and Viṣṇu-worship grew.

Signals agree on the churning role but **split on the deeper identity** (Vedic Prajāpati vs Vaiṣṇava Viṣṇu) — the disagreement is diachronic and is itself the finding → **contested**, confidence **medium**.
