## Gloss / Divergence map

**Jaimini** is the traditional author of the *Mīmāṃsā Sūtra* (Pūrva-Mīmāṃsā / "Karma-Mīmāṃsā"), the ~2,700-aphorism root text in twelve chapters that systematizes the interpretation of Vedic ritual (*karma-kāṇḍa*) and grounds an inquiry into *dharma*. He anchors the exegetical-ritualist darśana and its signature theses — the Veda as authorless (*apauruṣeya*), the eternality of word-meaning, and injunction (*vidhi*) as the core of meaning. The Mīmāṃsā commentarial spine runs **mūla (Jaimini) → Śabara-bhāṣya → Kumārila / Prabhākara**.

**Name-collision (teach in prose; no separate node).** The *philosopher* Jaimini must not be conflated with the *legendary epic* Jaimini — a disciple of Vyāsa to whom a Sāmaveda recension and the *Jaiminīya-Bhārata* are attached. Same name, different role; the attribution of the sūtra to the legendary figure is a traditional, not historical, identification.
