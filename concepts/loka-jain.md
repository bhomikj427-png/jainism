## Gloss / Divergence map

In Jain cosmology, *loka* is the **bounded world-space** — the finite region in which all six dravyas (substances) co-exist and interact. Beyond it stretches *aloka* (non-world-space), an infinite expanse of empty ākāśa (space) alone — no dharma/adharma (motion/rest media), no time, no souls, no matter.

**Loka vs. Aloka:**
- *Loka*: finite but enormous; contains all jīvas, all pudgala, all dharma/adharma/ākāśa/kāla
- *Aloka*: infinite; only pure ākāśa; nothing moves there (no dharma-dravya to enable motion); no jīva ever enters it

**Three divisions of loka:**

| region | Sanskrit | content |
|---|---|---|
| Lower world | *adholoka* | seven hells (*narakas*) with increasing suffering and duration |
| Middle world | *madhyaloka* | humans, animals, many gods on terrestrial planes; Jambudvīpa (central continent) |
| Upper world | *ūrdhvaloka* | heavens — multiple tiers of celestial realms, gods with increasing purity and lifespan |

*Siddha-śilā / lokāgra* (the "cap" of loka): sits at the very summit of loka, above the upper world — the permanent dwelling of liberated souls (*siddhas*). This is where jīvas that have attained mokṣa reside eternally in omniscient bliss. (Cross-reference: moksha.md.)

**The shape of loka** (traditional Jain cosmological imagery): depicted as a human figure (*loka-puruṣa*) — narrow at the bottom (the hells), broadest at the middle (human world), narrowing again toward the top, and the siddha-śilā a thin crescent at the apex.

**Why loka matters for the other concepts:**
- The *dharma* and *adharma* dravyas (media of motion and rest) exist only within loka — their function is to enable and restrain motion within loka; in aloka there is nothing to move and nothing to be at rest
- *Kāla* (time) also operates only within loka
- The liberated jīva rises through loka by the natural upward movement (free of karma's weight) and comes to rest at lokāgra — it does NOT exit into aloka

**TS Chapter coverage:** Chapters 3–4 of the TS provide detailed Jain cosmological geography (geography of middle world, classes of beings in all three worlds, lifespans, hells, heavens). These chapters are among the most extensive in the TS. Not directly fetched this run.
