## Gloss / Divergence map

**Vāmana** ("dwarf") is the **fifth of the [[avatara-vedanta|daśāvatāra]]** (after [[narasimha|Narasiṃha]]) and the first in human form. The Purāṇic story: the generous asura king **Bali** (Mahābali) has conquered the three worlds; Viṣṇu appears at Bali's sacrifice as a **dwarf brahmin** and begs only **three paces of land**. Bali grants it (against his guru Śukra's warning); Vāmana then swells to cosmic size — **Trivikrama**, "of the three strides" — covering the **earth** with one step and the **heavens** with the second; with nowhere left, Bali offers his own **head** for the third, and is pressed down to rule the netherworld (Pātāla/Sutala), the three worlds restored to Indra.

**The honest Vedic connection (a real historical descent, recorded as such, not a back-projection):**

| layer | content | source |
|---|---|---|
| Ṛgvedic | Viṣṇu measures the three worlds in **three strides** (*tri-vikrama*); **no dwarf, no Bali** | Ṛgveda 1.154 |
| Purāṇic | the three strides become the **dwarf-and-Bali** narrative | Purāṇas |

This is **not** the Prajāpati→Viṣṇu *reattribution* seen on [[matsya|Matsya]]/[[kurma|Kūrma]]/[[varaha|Varāha]]. Here the avatāra grows out of **Viṣṇu's own** Ṛgvedic act: "the Ṛgvedic passages referring to Viṣṇu's three strides are… the nucleus out of which the legend of the Dwarf was created" — but "neither Bali nor Vāmana appears in" the Vedic Trivikrama. The continuity is genuine; the dwarf-frame is the later accretion.

## Reasoning across signals

1. **Wikipedia, "Vamana"** — fifth of the daśāvatāra; Bali, the three paces, the Trivikrama expansion, the third step on Bali's head; the explicit statement that the Ṛgveda has the three strides but no dwarf — "the germ of the Dwarf Incarnation."
2. **Britannica, "Vamana"** (academic, via search) — independently: "Vāmana… begged as much land as he could step over in three paces… with one step covered the whole earth, and with the second step the midworld," sending Bali to the netherworld; and the Ṛgvedic Trivikrama base ("Tri-vikrama," "Uru-krama"), noting Bali/Vāmana are absent from the Vedic imagery.

Two independent sources agree on both the narrative and the Vedic-strides origin (with the dwarf as later development) → **converged**, confidence **medium**.
