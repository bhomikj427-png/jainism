## Gloss

Direct (pratyakṣa) knowledge of the thought-objects present in another person's mind — the fourth of five knowledge types, ranked above clairvoyance (avadhi) but below omniscience (kevala). "Manaḥ-paryāya" means "modes of mind": the knower perceives what physical modes (pudgala-paryāya) are being cognized in another's mind, not the mind-substance itself. Possession is restricted to human ascetics in the karma-regions (karma-bhūmi) who hold right faith and maintain self-discipline; it cannot arise in gods, hell-beings, or animals (who may have avadhi), because only humans in the proper condition develop the requisite karma-purification.

## Reasoning across signals

**Signal 1 — TS 1.23 (directly fetched, Vijay K. Jain 2018):** *ṛjuvipulamatī manaḥparyayaḥ* — "The two kinds of telepathy are ṛjumati and vipulamati." Exact Sanskrit confirmed.

**Signal 2 — Pūjyapāda, Sarvārthasiddhi on TS 1.23:** Both types arise through "destruction-cum-subsidence of energy-obstructing and telepathy-knowledge-obscuring karmas." Ṛjumati covers 2–8 births, up to one yojana spatially. Vipulamati covers 7 to innumerable births, up to the Mānuṣottara mountain range.

**Signal 3 — TS 1.12 (directly fetched, Vijay K. Jain 2018):** *pratyakṣamanyat* — manah-paryaya is classified as direct knowledge because it arises from the soul without sensory mediation.

**Signal 4 — WisdomLib (Manahparyaya, citing Jain canonical texts):** Only humans in karma-bhūmi can develop it; distinguishing mark of the two types is "purity and not being lost (apratipāta)."

All signals converge. Confidence medium: TS 1.23 fetched directly; Tatia 1994 chapter text not independently confirmed; no Digambara/Shvetambara difference specific to this concept found in sources consulted.

## Two types

| type | Sanskrit | scope (temporal) | scope (spatial) | key quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ṛjumati | ṛjumati | 2–8 past/future births of self and others | 1 gavyūti to 1 yojana | simpler range; liable to be lost |
| vipulamati | vipulamati | 7 to innumerable births | 1 yojana to Mānuṣottara range | purer; once acquired, never lost |

Vipulamati is the higher form: greater purity (viśuddhi) and infallibility (apratipāta = non-regression).

## Contrast with avadhi-jñāna

| feature | avadhi-jñāna | manaḥparyāya-jñāna |
|---|---|---|
| object known | physical matter (pudgala) directly | thought-objects (pudgala modes) as held in another's mind |
| who possesses it | gods/hell-beings (innate) + humans/animals (acquired) | only human ascetics with right faith in karma-bhūmi |
| rank in 5-knowledge ladder | 3rd | 4th (higher than avadhi) |
| acquisition | bhava-pratyaya (birth) or guṇa-pratyaya (karma purification) | guṇa-pratyaya only |
| spatial scope | varies widely | limited to human-inhabited regions |
