## Gloss / Divergence map

**Śeṣa** — also **Ananta** (अनन्त), Ādiśeṣa, Śeṣanāga — is the primordial cosmic serpent, eldest of [[kadru|Kadrū]]'s thousand nāga sons, who supports the earth on his hoods and serves as the thousand-headed couch on which [[vishnu|Viṣṇu]] reclines in the cosmic ocean (*kṣīrasāgara*) between world-cycles. His name encodes a precise theological point: *śeṣa* = "the remainder / that which remains" (from √śiṣ, "to remain"), because when the universe is annihilated at *pralaya* (the end of each *kalpa*), Śeṣa **alone remains** — the residual, indestructible substrate persisting across dissolution and re-creation. The epithet **Ananta** ("endless, infinite") names the same being from the complementary angle: his boundless, eternal extent. Recorded precisely and *not* modernized — this is a doctrine of the cosmic remnant, not a physics claim.

**Alias, not a second node.** `ananta` is a verified alias of Śeṣa, not a distinct figure: every independent source treats Ananta as an epithet of the same serpent (*śeṣa* = remainder; *ananta* = endlessness — two faces of one referent). No `ananta.md` is created; were a future text to use "Ananta" for a genuinely different referent, it would be split then with an `often-conflated-with-NOT-equivalent` edge.

## Reasoning across signals

1. **Viṣṇu Purāṇa** (Wilson translation tradition) — Viṣṇu "reposing, sleeping upon Śeṣa, in the midst of the deep."
2. **Mahābhārata + Bhāgavata Purāṇa + Garga Saṃhitā** (via wisdomlib's sourced excerpts) — earth-support; the explicit pralaya-remainder gloss ("when the universe is annihilated He alone remains").
3. **Wikipedia, "Shesha"** — the √śiṣ etymology and the Ananta-synonymy.

Three independent registers — Purāṇic translation, epic, and encyclopedic synthesis — agree on the gloss, the etymology, and the Ananta-alias; the remainder doctrine is explicitly textual, not inferred → **converged**, confidence **high**.
