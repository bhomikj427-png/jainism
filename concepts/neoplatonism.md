## Gloss / Divergence map

**Neoplatonism** is the late-antique systematising revival of Platonism founded by **Plotinus** (204/5–270 CE), whose treatises were arranged by his student **Porphyry** (c. 233–309) into the **Enneads** (six sets of nine). It is the school-overview anchor behind the doctrine file [[plotinus-one]], which treats its supreme principle in detail. The lineage continues through **Iamblichus** (c. 245–325, who centred **theurgy** — ritual ascent) and **Proclus** (412–485, who elaborated the system with intermediary *henads*).

**The hierarchy of hypostases.** Reality is a graded descent through three primary **hypostases**:

1. **the One** (= **the Good**) — absolutely simple, ineffable, **"beyond being" (epekeina tēs ousias)**, the source of all;
2. **Nous / Intellect** — the perfect image of the One, the realm of the Platonic **Forms** and eternal truths;
3. **Soul (Psyche)** — the mediating principle joining the intelligible to the sensible world.

**Emanation and return.** All things **proceed (proodos / emanation)** from the One "without diminishing the source — like heat radiating from fire," then **return (epistrophē)** toward it; the soul's goal is **henosis**, mystical union with the One, reached through contemplation (*theōria*) and ethical purification. Plotinus saw himself as a faithful interpreter of **Plato**, absorbing Aristotelian and Stoic elements rather than innovating.

**Reach.** Neoplatonism profoundly shaped **Christian** (Augustine, Pseudo-Dionysius), **Jewish** (Kabbalah), and **Islamic** (al-Kindī, Avicenna) philosophy and mysticism through the Middle Ages and Renaissance.

## Reasoning across signals

**Signal 1** (Wikipedia, "Neoplatonism"): Plotinus (c. 205–270) founder; Enneads compiled by Porphyry; Porphyry/Iamblichus(theurgy)/Proclus(henads); three hypostases — the One/Good ("ineffable, beyond being"), Nous (contains the Forms), World-Soul; emanation "without diminishing" the One; the soul's return / henosis; synthesis of Plato (+ Aristotle/Stoics); influence on Christian/Jewish/Islamic thought. **Signal 2** (SEP, "Plotinus"): 204/5–270; Enneads arranged by Porphyry into six-by-nine; the three hypostases (One/Good beyond being, Intellect/Forms, Soul); emanation as procession from the One that does not diminish it (the fire-and-heat image); proodos / epistrophē; henosis through theōria; founder of Neoplatonism; self-understood Platonist incorporating Aristotle and the Stoics.

The two are independent (a reference article and a peer-reviewed philosophy encyclopedia) and agree on dating, the Enneads, the three-hypostasis hierarchy, the One "beyond being," the emanation–return structure, henosis, and the Platonic self-understanding. **Medium, not high**: the Enneads were not fetched verbatim; the doctrinal detail of the One is cross-checked against the already-written [[plotinus-one]] rather than read from the text directly; later figures' (Proclus, Iamblichus) systems are summarised only.
