## Gloss

*Mohanīya* ("deluding karma") is the fourth and most consequential of the **eight karma species** (see karma-prakriti.md). Pūjyapāda's *Sarvārthasiddhi* calls it the karma that "makes the soul intoxicated like alcohol" — it corrupts the soul's discriminating capacity, generating *moha* (delusion-attachment) that drives all further influx. It is the **linchpin** of the four *ghāti* (destructive) karmas: once mohanīya is fully destroyed, the other three ghāti karmas (jñānāvaraṇa, darśanāvaraṇa, antarāya) collapse almost at once and omniscience (*kevala-jñāna*) dawns. The gunasthāna ladder (gunasthana.md) is essentially the staged demolition of mohanīya's 28 sub-types.

**The 28 sub-types** divide into two branches:

### 1. Darśana-mohanīya — 3 sub-types (clouds right faith)

| sub-type | what it does |
|---|---|
| *mithyātva* | wrong belief; inverts the soul's grasp of truth |
| *samyaktva-mithyātva* | mixed right-and-wrong belief; unstable oscillation |
| *samyaktva* (subsidential) | quiescent state where wrong-belief is suppressed but not eradicated |

### 2. Cāritra-mohanīya — 25 sub-types (clouds right conduct)

**Kaṣāya-vedanīya (passion-causing) — 16:** four passions × four intensities (TS 8.9):

| intensity | binds to | eliminated at gunasthāna |
|---|---|---|
| *anantānubandhī* (infinite-binding) | prevents right faith itself | 4 (right faith arises) |
| *apratyākhyāna* (prevents-partial-renunciation) | blocks lay vows | 5 (aṇuvrata level) |
| *pratyākhyāna* (prevents-full-renunciation) | blocks monastic vows | 6 (mahāvrata level) |
| *saṃjvalana* (smoldering) | residual passion; delays mokṣa | 10 (subtle-passion only) |

Each intensity applies to all four kaṣāyas: *krodha* (anger), *māna* (pride), *māyā* (deceit), *lobha* (greed) = 4 × 4 = **16 types**.

**No-kaṣāya-vedanīya (quasi-passion-causing) — 9:** secondary emotional reactions — *hāsya* (laughter), *rati* (liking), *arati* (disliking), *śoka* (sorrow), *bhaya* (fear), *jugupsā* (disgust), *strī-veda* (female-sex-passion), *puṃ-veda* (male-sex-passion), *napuṃsaka-veda* (neuter-sex-passion). These are eliminated at gunasthāna 12 (before mohanīya's final eradication).

**Total: 3 + 16 + 9 = 28 sub-types** (matching the karma-prakriti.md note that mohanīya has 28 of the traditional 148 uttara-prakṛtis).

## Reasoning across signals

**Signal 1 — TS 8.4 (Vijay K. Jain 2018, fetched directly via karma-prakriti.md):** Names mohanīya as the fourth of the eight karma species; confirmed ghāti-family placement.

**Signal 2 — TS 8.9 (Vijay K. Jain 2018, WisdomLib doc1084874, fetched):** Verse establishes the four-intensity structure of cāritra-mohanīya: *"anantānubandhyapratyākhyānapratyākhyānasaṃjvalana vikalpāścaikaśaḥ krodhamānamāyālobhāḥ"* — four intensity-grades, each in four passion-kinds. Pūjyapāda's commentary distinguishes darśana-mohanīya (faith-deluding) as producing three states of wrong/mixed/quiescent belief, and cāritra-mohanīya (conduct-deluding) as producing kaṣāyas and no-kaṣāyas.

**Signal 3 — Wikipedia "Types of Karma (Jainism)":** Independent secondary source confirms the 28-sub-type structure (3 + 25 = 3 darśana + 16 kaṣāya + 9 no-kaṣāya) and the linchpin status. Not a critical edition — treated as corroboration only.

**Independence note:** Signal 1 and Signal 2 are from the same Vijay K. Jain 2018 edition (not fully independent); Signal 3 is an independent secondary source. Confidence remains medium: the 9 no-kaṣāya list and the 4-intensity-table are from TS 8.9 and commentary, but the precise mapping of "eliminated at which gunasthāna" is drawn from the broader Jain commentarial tradition, not from a single directly-fetched verse.
