## Gloss / Divergence map

**Rāhu** is the **severed head** of the asura **Svarbhānu**. During the **[[samudra-manthana|Samudra-Manthana]]**, while **[[mohini|Mohinī]]** distributes the **[[amrita|amṛta]]**, he disguises himself among the gods to steal a sip; Sūrya and Candra (sun and moon) expose him, and Viṣṇu beheads him with the discus — but the nectar has already reached his throat, so the **head is immortal** while the body dies. In jyotiṣa Rāhu is reckoned a **graha** (one of the *navagraha*, the nine "planets") and identified with the **ascending (north) lunar node**; his severed body/tail becomes **[[ketu|Ketu]]**, the descending node.

**A genuine conflation to keep apart (§0 — analogy is not identity):**

| layer | what it asserts |
|---|---|
| **myth** | Rāhu, the headless immortal, periodically **swallows** the sun and moon (who slip out of his open neck) — the narrative cause of eclipses |
| **astronomy (siddhānta)** | eclipses occur at the **lunar nodes**, where the moon's path crosses the ecliptic; classical Indian astronomers (e.g. Āryabhaṭa) gave a shadow/geometry account |

The two coexisted: the node-points took the **names** Rāhu and Ketu, and the eclipse-myth was *correlated* with the nodal geometry. But the swallowing-demon story is **not itself** an astronomical theory of eclipses, and reading it as one (or as "the ancients secretly knew the nodes") collapses a mythic etiology into a mathematical model they held separately. The relationship is one of **naming and correlation**, not identity.

## Reasoning across signals

1. **Wikipedia, "Rahu"** — severed head of Svarbhānu; beheading during the churning after the stolen amṛta; navagraha status; ascending lunar node; the eclipse-by-swallowing legend; Ketu as the descending node; the ~18-year cycle matching nodal precession.
2. **Mahābhārata, Ādi Parva (Ganguli tr.)** — independently: Rāhu, in the guise of a god, drinks the nectar; the sun and moon inform Viṣṇu; his head is severed by the discus — the perpetual sun/moon-and-Rāhu enmity (eclipses) established here.

Encyclopedia and critical-translation agree on the beheading and the eclipse-enmity; the astronomical node-identification is the jyotiṣa systematization → **converged**, confidence **medium** (the myth↔astronomy relation is correlation, recorded as such).
