## Gloss / Divergence map

**Uccaiḥśravas** ("[the horse] of loud neighing / long ears") is the **seven-headed white flying horse**, "the best of horses," that rises as one of the **ratnas of the [[samudra-manthana|Samudra-Manthana]]**. It is taken by **Indra** (some texts assign it to the asura king **Bali**). Its narrative importance is the **wager over its tail**: the sisters **[[kadru|Kadrū]]** and **[[vinata|Vinatā]]** bet on whether the horse's tail is white or black; Kadrū has her serpent-sons darken it to win, enslaving Vinatā — the quarrel that drives the serpent-saga and the birth of **[[garuda|Garuḍa]]**, who labors to free his mother. So Uccaiḥśravas is both a churning-treasure and the **hinge of the Kadrū–Vinatā cycle**.

A low-controversy node: origin and the wager are stable; only the owner (Indra vs Bali) varies by text.
