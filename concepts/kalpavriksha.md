## Gloss / Divergence map

The **Kalpavṛkṣa** (also *kalpataru*, *kalpadruma*, *kalpapādapa*) is the divine **wish-fulfilling tree** that emerges from the Samudra-Manthana (the churning of the ocean of milk) among the *ratnas*/treasures, alongside [[kamadhenu|Kāmadhenu]] the wish-fulfilling cow; Indra takes it to his heaven. It grants whatever is desired — a mythic figure of inexhaustible abundance, iconographically described with jewelled parts (golden roots, coral leaves, gemstone fruit). In one tradition there are **five** such trees in Indra's gardens — Mandāna, Pārijāta, Santāna, Kalpavṛkṣa, Haricandana — so "Kalpavṛkṣa" names both the class and one specific member. It pairs naturally with Kāmadhenu: the wish-tree and the wish-cow as twin churning-born emblems of divine wish-fulfilment.

**Variant (recorded, not reconciled).** Pārijāta is sometimes treated as *the* churning-tree or as a Kalpavṛkṣa member, conflating two distinct named trees; the five-trees enumeration is itself tradition-dependent.
