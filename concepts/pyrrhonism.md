## Gloss / Divergence map

**Pyrrhonism** is the ancient Greek **sceptical** tradition that takes its name from **Pyrrho of Elis** (c. 365–275 BCE) and is preserved chiefly in the *Outlines of Pyrrhonism* of **Sextus Empiricus** (2nd–3rd c. CE). Its goal is not a doctrine but a *therapy*: by suspending judgment one arrives at tranquillity.

**The core mechanism — epochē via isostheneia.** Scepticism is "an ability to set out oppositions among things which appear and are thought of in any way at all." For any question the sceptic constructs opposed arguments of **equal strength** (*isostheneia* / equipollence); finding the two sides balanced, one cannot assent to either, so judgment is **suspended** (*epochē*). The suspension is generated by the **Modes (tropes)**: the **Ten Modes of Aenesidemus** (perceptual and cultural relativity — honey is sweet to the healthy, bitter to the jaundiced; animals, circumstances, distances, customs all differ) and the **Five Modes of Agrippa** (dispute, infinite regress, relativity, hypothesis, reciprocity/circularity), which attack the *structure* of justification itself.

**The pay-off — ataraxia.** The sceptic originally sought tranquillity (*ataraxia*) by *settling* questions, but found it arrives only *after* suspension, unsought, "following epochē like a shadow." Sextus's image is the painter **Apelles**: unable to paint a horse's lather, he threw his sponge at the panel in frustration — and the random smear produced exactly the lather. Tranquillity comes when you stop trying to pin reality down.

**No thesis, only appearances.** The Pyrrhonist asserts **no dogma** about how things really are; he lives *adoxastōs* (without belief) by the **appearances** (*phainomena*) — when chilled he does not deny he is chilled — while withholding assent to any theoretical claim about their hidden nature. This is the crux distinguishing Pyrrhonism from **Academic** scepticism: the Academics *dogmatically assert* that nothing can be known (itself a knowledge-claim); the Pyrrhonist suspends even on *that*, and keeps investigating.

**Pyrrho himself.** Pyrrho wrote nothing; he is reconstructed from his follower Timon via the **Aristocles passage** (transmitted by Eusebius). It reports that things are by nature *adiaphora* (undifferentiable), *astathmēta* (unstable/unmeasurable), and *anepikrita* (indeterminable); that one should therefore be unopinionated and use the formula that a thing "**no more** is than is not, or both is and is not, or neither is nor is not"; and that the result is *aphasia* (non-assertion) and then *ataraxia*. Whether this describes reality (metaphysical reading) or only our access to it (epistemological reading) is disputed.

## Reasoning across signals

**Signal 1** (SEP, "Sextus Empiricus"): epochē from equipollence/isostheneia; the Ten Modes (Aenesidemus, relativity) and Five Modes (Agrippa, justification-structure); ataraxia as the unintended sequel to suspension, with the Apelles story and the "shadow" image; the Pyrrhonist/Academic contrast (Academics make the dogmatic claim "no discovery can be made"); the dogma distinction in *PH* I 13 (acquiescing in appearances vs assenting to unclear theoretical objects). **Signal 2** (SEP, "Pyrrho"): the Aristocles passage and its three questions (nature → attitude → result); the *adiaphora/astathmēta/anepikrita* triad; the "no more (ou mallon)" tetralemma-shaped formula; *aphasia* → *ataraxia*; that Pyrrho wrote nothing and is mediated through Timon.

The two SEP entries are independent within the same reference work (different authors, different primary bases — Sextus's extant corpus vs the fragmentary Pyrrho testimonia) and converge on the structure. Medium, not high: the primary texts (Sextus's *PH*; the Aristocles passage in Eusebius's *Praeparatio Evangelica*) were not fetched verbatim; the metaphysical-vs-epistemological reading of Pyrrho is genuinely unsettled. The frequently-cited claim that Pyrrho's thought was shaped by his travels to India with Alexander is noted in the literature but is **speculative** and is deliberately **not** used here to assert any influence either way (doing so would be exactly the false-equivalence trap §0 forbids).
