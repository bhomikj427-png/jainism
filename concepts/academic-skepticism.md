## Gloss / Divergence map

**Academic skepticism** designates the Platonic Academy's skeptical period (~266–90 BCE), when its leaders denied that certain knowledge is attainable and advocated *epochē* (suspension of assent) on theoretical claims. It divides into two phases:

| figure | dates | key doctrine |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcesilaus** of Pitane | c. 316–241 BCE; head of Academy ~266 BCE | Radical *epochē* on all claims; attacked the Stoic *kataleptikē phantasia* (infallible grasp-impression); "strength of conviction is not a mark of truth — contradictory beliefs feel equally convincing"; no practical criterion offered |
| **Carneades** of Cyrene | 219–129 BCE; New Academy | *To pithanon* (the probable / plausible): truth exists but is inaccessible; degrees of probability guide practice ("plausible in itself" → "plausible and uncontradicted" → "plausible, uncontradicted, and thoroughly investigated"); written nothing himself — known via Clitomachus |

**Critical distinction from Pyrrhonism** (the other Greek skepticism):
- *Academics*: knowledge is impossible, but truth *exists*; we can make probabilistic judgments; targeted attack on Stoic certainty.
- *Pyrrhonists*: suspend judgment on *everything*, including the claim that truth exists; ataraxia (tranquillity) follows from universal suspension.
- Same form (epochē), different scope and outcome.

**End of the school**: Antiochus of Ascalon (~90 BCE) rejected skepticism, returned the Academy to dogmatism, and initiated Middle Platonism. The movement lasted ~175 years.

**Grounding in Plato/Socrates**: Academics cited *Phaedo* (Socrates: "knowledge is not accessible to mortals") and Socratic *elenchus* (refutation without positive doctrine) as their intellectual warrant — though modern scholars generally dispute that Plato was himself a skeptic.

1. **Wikipedia "Academic Skepticism"**: defines the period (266–90 BCE); Arcesilaus's anti-kataleptic argument; Carneades's *pithanon* doctrine with its three-grade scale; distinction from Pyrrhonism (Academics acknowledge truth exists; Pyrrhonists suspend even that); Antiochus's termination.
