# chapters/hindu/shastra/

**Technical / scientific śāstra** chapters land here — the systematic disciplines that are
neither philosophy (*darśana*), nor deity-cult, nor scripture: medicine (Āyurveda),
and any future coverage of astronomy/astrology (*jyotiṣa*), statecraft (*Arthaśāstra*),
dramaturgy (*Nāṭyaśāstra*), grammar (*vyākaraṇa*), and the like.

Sibling sub-folders under `hindu/`:
- `darsana/` — the philosophical systems (the āstika darśanas: Vedānta, Sāṃkhya, Yoga, Nyāya, Vaiśeṣika, Mīmāṃsā).
- `devotional/` — deities, vāhanas, iconography, bhakti, the deity-families.
- `scripture/` — the epics and canonical texts (Vedas, Upaniṣads, Mahābhārata, Rāmāyaṇa, Gītā, the root sūtras).

**Why this folder exists.** Ch. 23 (Āyurveda) fits none of the three existing sub-folders:
the Saṃhitās are texts but not scripture, the system is systematic but not a darśana, and
Dhanvantari's patronage is a religious framing laid over a medical tradition rather than the
tradition itself. Rather than force it, `shastra/` was added — the same reasoning that gave
`hindu/` its second level in the first place: "Hindu" spans genuinely different domains and
a flat folder would not scale.

See `chapters/INDEX.md` for the live chapter map.
