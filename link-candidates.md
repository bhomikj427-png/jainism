## Policy decision recorded this pass (read before "fixing reciprocals")

A full audit found **~210 symmetric edges that exist in only one direction**
(`structurally-parallel-to`, `shares-vocabulary-with`,
`often-conflated-with-NOT-equivalent`). **These are NOT defects.** CLAUDE.md §5
is explicit: *"Only forward links are stored; backlinks are computed with grep,
never hand-maintained."* A one-directional symmetric edge is by design — the
reverse is recovered by `grep`, not stored. Do **not** mass-add ~210 reciprocals;
that would nearly double the edge count and violate §5.

Reciprocals are added **only** when they fix a real connectivity defect — i.e.
an orphan node (in-degree 0) that is unreachable on forward traversal. That is
the linker's actual job, alongside pairing-rule / direction compliance and
integrating newly written concepts.
